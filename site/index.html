[01] How it works ////////

Hide your face on camera in three steps.
All of them on your machine.

  1. 01

    Detect

    An on-device model finds 478 points on your face. A second one keeps track of your head when you look down or turn away.

  2. 02

    Replace

    Your face becomes a mask that follows your expressions, a glitch, or a full-head cover. Lose tracking and the frame goes dark until you're found again.

  3. 03

    Go live

    Pick the n0face virtual camera in Discord, Zoom, Teams or OBS. Or drop in photos and videos to mask or blur every face, with EXIF and GPS metadata stripped.

[03] Modes ////////

Mask, blur or anonymize.
Pick how hidden you need to be.

We'll be straight with you: no mode makes you unidentifiable. Your voice, body and room can still give you away. Here's exactly what each one does.

01

MASK

Decorative

Stylized masks over a blur of your face, or a solid backing. Shows your expressions.

Doesn't hide your facial geometry or expressions.

02

OBFUSCATE

Hides your face

Replaces your face with glitch blocks or static, generated from scratch, or pixelates it.

Doesn't hide face outline, hair, ears, body or background.

03

ANONYMIZE

Strongest

Covers your whole head with a shape-hiding cover and holds it longer when tracking drops.

Doesn't hide body, voice, background or anything you say.

[04] Privacy ////////

Private by construction,
not by promise.

An offline face anonymizer: detection, masking and the virtual camera all run on your PC, and the code is open for anyone to check.

  • On-device

    Detection runs on your CPU. Frames never leave your PC.

  • Network off

    Once the model loads, n0face switches its own networking off.

  • Fail-closed

    If tracking drops for more than a few frames, the whole frame is hidden.

  • No recording

    Live camera frames are never written to disk.

  • Metadata stripped

    Masked copies of photos and videos carry no EXIF or GPS. Originals are never changed.

  • Open source

    Every line is on GitHub under AGPL-3.0. Read it, build it yourself.

[06] FAQ ////////

Questions, answered.

Something missing? Ask on GitHub.

How do I hide my face on stream or in a video call?

Install n0face, pick a mask or privacy mode, and choose the n0face camera in Discord, Zoom, Teams, Google Meet or OBS. Your face is masked before any app sees the picture. The virtual camera needs the free OBS Studio installed (it provides the driver); OBS doesn't have to be running.

Is n0face a Snap Camera alternative?

If you miss Snap Camera's face filters on your webcam: yes, n0face is a free virtual camera with 3D face masks that works in the same apps. The difference is that it's built for privacy: it runs offline, has no account and never uploads your camera.

Can I blur or mask faces in photos and videos?

Yes. Drop in photos or videos and n0face writes masked copies, with EXIF, GPS and other metadata stripped. Your originals are never changed. Video processing runs on your CPU and takes roughly ten times the video's length.

Is it good for faceless YouTube, TikTok or Twitch content?

That's a big part of why it exists. Stream through OBS with a mask on, or record normally and mask the video afterwards. A second on-device model keeps your head covered when you look down at a guitar, keyboard or phone.

Does n0face work offline? Is my webcam footage uploaded?

Everything runs on your PC. Once the face model has loaded, n0face switches its own networking off. There's no account, no cloud and no telemetry, and live camera frames are never written to disk.

Will n0face make me anonymous?

Not on its own, and no tool can promise that. ANONYMIZE covers your whole head and is the strongest mode, but your voice, body, clothes, room and what you say can still identify you. MASK mode is decorative: it shows your expressions.

Is n0face free?

Yes. n0face is free and open source under AGPL-3.0, including every privacy feature. The optional n0face PRO adds cosmetic extras: more masks, accessories and customization.

What do I need to run it?

Windows 10 or 11 and a webcam. The installer bundles everything else, including the face model. Add OBS Studio if you want the virtual camera in other apps.
